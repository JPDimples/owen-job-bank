# Owen's Job Bank — Programs & Schooling Research Notes

Checked: **2026-11-16**. All facts below were checked against the linked source pages on that date.
Read-only research — no applications submitted, no programs contacted.

## What was verified

- **Minimum wage (Part A): CONFIRMED.** General $17.95/hr, student (under 18) $16.90/hr,
  effective 2026-10-01 to 2027-09-30, from the live ontario.ca ESA guide. The existing chart's
  figures were correct. Student rate applies only at ≤28 hrs/week while school is in session
  (or any hours during a school break); over 28 hrs/week in term time, the general rate applies.
- **Canada Summer Jobs 2027: CONFIRMED OPEN.** Employer applications 2026-10-27 → 2026-12-03
  (11:59 p.m. PT), youth jobs 2027-04-19 → 2027-08-28, ages 15-30. Verified on canada.ca
  (program page, applicant guide, Oct 27 news release). Summer-only, full-time — not an
  after-school option, but Owen's best summer-2027 target alongside OPS student jobs.
- **YESS: CONFIRMED as an umbrella strategy** (ages 15-30, citizen/PR/refugee, legally entitled
  to work). No central application or deadline; delivered by local providers. Most projects
  target out-of-school youth facing barriers, so limited value for Owen now.
- **Youth Job Connection Summer: CONFIRMED as the most relevant Ontario program** (ages 15-18,
  in high school, returning to school, facing challenging life circumstances; 20 hrs paid
  pre-employment training, summer job up to 8 weeks, up to 10 hrs/week evenings/weekends in
  term time). Verified via Employment Ontario Partners' Gateway + ontario.ca. **But the
  year-round YJC stream is likely NOT for Owen** — it requires being out of school and unemployed.
- **OPS Summer Employment Opportunities (SEO): CONFIRMED.** 2026 closed; 2027 postings expected
  fall/winter 2026, ages 15+, student status required. Verified on ontario.ca (updated 2026-10-16).
- **Summer Company: CONFIRMED as a program** (ages 15-29, returning student, up to $3,000).
  2027 intake reported as 2027-01-25 → 2027-05-21 by local delivery centres — **not yet
  confirmed on an ontario.ca page**.
- **Stewardship Youth Ranger Program: CONFIRMED** (ages 16-17, 8 weeks summer, may earn 2 co-op
  credits; postings March-April). Only relevant if Owen is 16+ by summer 2027 — his exact age
  was not stated, so no eligibility claim is made.
- **OYAP: CONFIRMED** on ontario.ca (15+, Grade 11/12, 14+ credits; OYAP-FAST accelerated stream
  since Sept 2025; apply via guidance/co-op teacher + board recruiter).
- **High-school co-op: CONFIRMED** (Grade 11/12 after Grade 10, 2- or 4-credit, unpaid, earns
  OSSD credits, free, via the school's guidance/co-op department). No single ontario.ca co-op
  page was found; verified via school-board sources + the OYAP page.
- **Durham College dual credit: CONFIRMED** (free to student, via guidance; partner boards
  include DDSB and DCDSB; Centre for Success Feb 2027 applications listed open — Grade 12,
  24+ credits only).
- **Bronze Cross / lifeguard pathway: CONFIRMED** (Bronze Medallion 13+, Bronze Cross 13+ with
  Medallion + first aid, National Lifeguard 15+ with Bronze Cross + Standard/Intermediate First
  Aid). Local delivery confirmed: Municipality of Clarington (Bowmanville) runs the courses
  and hires Assistant Lifeguards (Bronze Cross minimum).
- **Standard/Intermediate First Aid: CONFIRMED**, including the WSIB rename (Standard → Intermediate,
  Emergency → Basic) effective 2026-06-22, 3-year certification, local provider Durham First Aid
  (Oshawa, Red Cross Training Partner).
- **Food Handler: CONFIRMED** (O. Reg. 493/17 s.32, 70% pass, 5-year validity, recognized by all
  Ontario health units incl. Durham Region; online ~$25-50, in-person ~$40-62).
- **Smart Serve: CONFIRMED NOT USABLE UNTIL 18.** Full course $44.95 / Retail $24.95, 80% pass,
  proctored, 5-year validity — verified directly on smartserve.ca. Minimum age 18 to sell/serve/
  handle alcohol in Ontario (AGCO). Do not list as a near-term option for Owen.

## Dead-ends / things that are NOT what they seem

- **Ontario Tech University has NO dual-credit program found.** Its high-school offering is
  paid outreach/enrichment (Inside Engineering, Future Leaders in Training) and both 2026-27
  flagship programs are already CLOSED for registration. Do not claim a dual-credit route there.
- **Youth Job Link: status UNKNOWN, likely defunct/renamed.** It appears in older Employment
  Ontario material but is NOT on the current ontario.ca youth-jobs page (checked 2026-11-16).
  Listed in programs.json with verified=false — do not present it as available.
- **YESS is not a job board or a single application.** Youth join a project run by a funded local
  organization; there is no central YESS application for Owen to submit.
- **Canada Summer Jobs is employer-applied.** Owen cannot apply to CSJ; he applies to the funded
  jobs when they appear on Job Bank in spring 2027.
- **Smart Serve conflicting secondary prices ($34.95 / $39.49 / $45) are all stale or wrong.**
  The live official price on smartserve.ca on 2026-11-16 is $44.95 (Full) / $24.95 (Retail).
- **'Standard First Aid' listings may now say 'Intermediate First Aid'** after the June 2026
  WSIB rename — same course, new name. Old pages using the old name are not wrong, just dated.
- **Co-op is unpaid by design.** Do not present co-op placements as paid jobs; the value is
  credits, experience, and a foot in the door with a local employer.
